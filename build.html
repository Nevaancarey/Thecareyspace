What I Build

Hands-on hardware projects — UAVs, flight controllers, and embedded systems built, broken, and rebuilt along the way.

APR 2026

Build Guild — Micro Drone

Built, calibrated, and flight-tested a micro drone prototype with stable autonomous altitude hold and orientation-independent control. 2nd place finish.

OCT 2025 – JAN 2026

SonoStep: Assistive Navigation System

Raspberry Pi–based smart cane with a camera-based vision system for obstacle detection, real-time audio feedback, and GPS point-to-point navigation.

NOV 2024 – JAN 2025

FPV Quadcopter with Pixhawk

Resolved key performance issues in a prior UAV by transitioning from Arduino to Pixhawk 2.4.8, adding GPS, telemetry, and AV transmitters.

JUN 2024 – NOV 2024

"Slow Fly" RC Plane

Developed and tested a low-speed RC plane using a 1000KV BLDC motor, 30A ESC, and SG90 servos — built for the S.P.A.R.K. Expo 2024.

AUG 2023 – NOV 2023

Quadcopter using Arduino Microcontroller

My most challenging early build — Arduino isn't suited for stable flight control, but after months of iteration, produced a working flying quadcopter.
