Who I Am

I'm Nevaan Carey Meduri, a Cambridge A Level student passionate about technology, problem-solving, and creating solutions that make a real impact. My interests lie at the intersection of robotics, electronics, and artificial intelligence. From building drones and embedded systems to developing assistive devices like SonoStep for the visually impaired, I love turning ideas into practical innovations.

Beyond academics, I believe in giving back. For the past two years, I've mentored 30+ students from a rural government school, introducing them to STEM through hands-on workshops in electronics, robotics, and drone technology — helping them dream bigger and build confidence.

As Head of Stewardship at my school, I've had the privilege of leading initiatives that promote responsibility, sustainability, and community service. I'm a lifelong learner, constantly exploring new technologies and challenging myself to grow.

My goal is to become a space robotics scientist and be part of pioneer innovations that propel humanity forward.
