05 — About

About Me

I'm a high school student with a deep interest in aerospace systems, space robotics, and applied engineering — with a strong focus on building, leading, and deploying real-world solutions.

My work spans UAV systems, embedded electronics, and space science research: from building quadcopters and RC aircraft, to leading Project S.H.A.N.I., where I worked with an international team to design a conceptual sampling system for Titan's surface. I've also researched space debris mitigation, analyzing mission architectures and long-term sustainability challenges in orbit.

Beyond technical projects, I care about applying engineering for social impact. As founder of a rural STEM initiative, I design and deliver hands-on electronics and robotics curricula to underserved students — bridging the gap between rural communities and access to opportunity.

Looking ahead, I hope to pursue engineering at a leading global institution and contribute to projects that advance technology while transforming lives.
